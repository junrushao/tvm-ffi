# 9a1d41a — Revert "[FFI] Replace `Arg2Str` with a more powerful `for_each`" (#18121)

## TL;DR
- Full revert of commit `5a4038f` (`[FFI] Replace Arg2Str with a more powerful for_each`).
- Restores `Arg2Str<T>` in `function_details.h` and reverts `for_each_dispatcher` to the simple fold expression form without multi-invocation dispatch.

## Why (intent / motivation)
- The enhanced `for_each` using `std::conjunction_v` over complex constraint packs caused compilation issues on some supported platforms/compilers.
- Reverted to restore build stability.

## What changed (facts from diff)
- `base_details.h`: reverts to simple `(f(I, ...), ...)` fold expression in `for_each_dispatcher::run`; removes `always_false`, `type_traits` include, and multi-branch `if constexpr`.
- `function_details.h`: restores `Arg2Str<T>` struct and its usage in `FuncFunctorImpl::Sig()`.
