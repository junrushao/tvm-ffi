# 53a7fe9 — [PYTHON] Robustify libinfo finding (#185)

## TL;DR
- Wraps the `path.resolve()` call in `get_dll_directories()` in a try/except to silently skip paths that raise `OSError` (e.g., inaccessible directories).

## Why (intent / motivation)
- On some systems, `Path.resolve()` can raise `OSError` (permission denied or broken symlinks) when iterating `PATH` entries, causing library discovery to fail entirely.

## What changed (facts from diff)
- `python/tvm_ffi/libinfo.py`: In `get_dll_directories()`, the single-line list comprehension is replaced with a loop that catches `OSError` around `path.resolve()`.
