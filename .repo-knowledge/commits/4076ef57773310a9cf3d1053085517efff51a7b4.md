# 4076ef5 — Workaround Segfaults and clang-tidy warnings (#297)

## TL;DR
- Adds a clang-tidy suppression comment to `function_details.h` to address a false-positive `clang-analyzer-cplusplus.NewDeleteLeaks` warning
- Adds explicit `gc.collect()` and `del mod` to `test_stl.py` to work around potential use-after-free segfaults on mainline CI

## Why (intent / motivation)
- After merging PR #228 (STL support), segfaults appeared on mainline CI; investigation pointed to module lifetime vs Python GC ordering; adding explicit cleanup mitigated the issue

## What changed (facts from diff)
- `include/tvm/ffi/function_details.h`: Added `// NOLINTNEXTLINE(clang-analyzer-cplusplus.NewDeleteLeaks` comment after `unpack_call`
- `tests/python/test_stl.py`: Added `import gc`, `gc.collect()`, `del mod` at end of test to ensure module is unloaded after all dependent objects are freed
