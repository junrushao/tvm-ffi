# 33b3768 — [CMAKE] Hide symbols from libbacktrace (#139)

## TL;DR
- Adds `-fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden` compile flags when building `libbacktrace` on GCC, Clang, and AppleClang.
- Prevents libbacktrace symbols from leaking into the public symbol table of `libtvm_ffi`.

## Why (intent / motivation)
- libbacktrace is an internal dependency; its symbols should not be exported to avoid name collisions with other libraries that may include libbacktrace.

## What changed (facts from diff)
- `cmake/Utils/AddLibbacktrace.cmake`: Added compiler detection for GCC/Clang/AppleClang; adds symbol hiding flags to `CFLAGS` of the ExternalProject.
