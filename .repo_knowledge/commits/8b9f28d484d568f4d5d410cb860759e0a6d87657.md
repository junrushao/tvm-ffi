# 8b9f28d -- fix(build): Link dl and pthread dependencies for tvm_ffi (#360)

## TL;DR
- Adds `Threads::Threads` (pthread) and `${CMAKE_DL_LIBS}` (dl) as link dependencies for the tvm_ffi shared and static targets.
- Fixes linking failures on platforms where pthread and dl are not implicitly linked.

## Why (intent / motivation)
- Resolves issue #344. `std::mutex` in `src/ffi/backtrace.cc` requires pthread, and `dlsym`/`dlopen` in `src/ffi/extra/library_module_dynamic_lib.cc` requires libdl. Without explicit linking, builds could fail on certain Linux configurations.

## What changed (facts from diff)
- `CMakeLists.txt`: Added `find_package(Threads REQUIRED)` near the top.
- `CMakeLists.txt`: `tvm_ffi_shared` and `tvm_ffi_static` now link with `Threads::Threads`.
- `CMakeLists.txt`: When `TVM_FFI_USE_EXTRA_CXX_API` is enabled and `CMAKE_DL_LIBS` is available, `tvm_ffi_shared` and `tvm_ffi_static` also link with dl.
