# Skill /commit-distill - Self-Evolution
- **Template mismatch**: None.
- **Upstream skill gap**: Several ledgers had empty `scope:` YAML fields (no list entries), making triage harder. The frontmatter triage in Step 1.5 had to rely on subject text parsing instead. `/commit-ledger` should always populate `scope` even for trivial commits.
- **Quality bar friction**: None -- the batch had sufficient feature commits to meet all quality bars naturally.
- **Procedure friction**: Step 1.5 batch triage says to read "only YAML frontmatter" but does not specify how many lines to read. For commits with multi-line scope lists, 20 lines was insufficient. A clearer line limit or "read until `---`" instruction would help.
- **Suggested skill change**: In Step 1.5, replace "read only the YAML frontmatter" with "read until the closing `---` marker (typically first 15-25 lines)" for clarity.
