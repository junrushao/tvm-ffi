- Added `NOLINTNEXTLINE(bugprone-unchecked-optional-access)` to `TypedOverload::CallAux` where `std::optional` dereference is intentionally unchecked (the optional is guaranteed populated by the preceding `TrySetOne` call chain).
- Added a Doxygen `\brief` comment for the `using Super = ObjectDef<Class>` alias in `OverloadObjectDef` to satisfy documentation linting.
