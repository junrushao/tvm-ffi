# Rewrite Quick Start guide from Markdown to RST with polished structure and multi-framework examples
## TL;DR
- Deleted `docs/get_started/quick_start.md` (230 lines) and replaced it with `docs/get_started/quickstart.rst` (329 lines), a completely rewritten RST guide.
- The new guide is restructured into three clear sections: "Write a Simple `add_one`" (with C++/CUDA tabs), "Ship Across ML Frameworks" (PyTorch/JAX/NumPy/CuPy tabs), and "Ship Across Languages" (Python/C++/Rust), plus a troubleshooting appendix.
- Updated cross-references in `docs/index.rst` and `docs/guides/python_guide.md` to point to the new filename.
