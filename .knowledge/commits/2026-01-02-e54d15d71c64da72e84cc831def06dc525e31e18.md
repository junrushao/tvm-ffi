# Add bounds checking for size() and stride() methods
## TL;DR
- Adds bounds checking with IndexError to Tensor::size(), Tensor::stride(), TensorView::size(), and TensorView::stride() methods.
- Handles both positive and negative index out-of-range cases.

None (behavioral fix to existing API)

## Impact
