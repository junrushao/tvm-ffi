# Trigger wheel builds on every push to main, keeping publish gated to manual workflow_dispatch

## TL;DR
- `publish_wheel.yml` now triggers on every `push` to `main` in addition to `workflow_dispatch`, enabling continuous wheel-build smoke tests.
- Both checkout steps updated: `ref:` uses `inputs.branch` for manual runs and `github.ref` for push-triggered runs.
- The upload/publish job remains gated to `workflow_dispatch` — wheels built on `main` pushes are artifacts only, not published to PyPI.
