# Fix clang-tidy warnings with NOLINT annotations and a const-ref fix in reflection registry
## TL;DR
- Adds `NOLINT`/`NOLINTBEGIN`/`NOLINTEND` annotations to suppress clang-tidy warnings across 5 files: `clang-analyzer-security.ArrayBound` in `StableHashBytes`, `modernize-macro-to-enum` for version macros in `c_api.h`, `performance-unnecessary-value-param` for template iterators in `array.h` and `access_path.h`, `bugprone-reserved-identifier` and `bugprone-casting-through-void` in `testing.cc`.
- Fixes one genuine issue: changes `const Class target` (pass-by-value) to `const Class& target` in `ReflectionDefBase::def_method` wrapper lambda in `registry.h`.
