# Fix compatibility with PyTorch <= 2.8 for Float8_e8m0fnu and Float4
## TL;DR
- Extends the `#if TORCH_VERSION_MAJOR >= 2 && TORCH_VERSION_MINOR >= 8` guard in `_optional_torch_c_dlpack.py` to also cover `Float8_e8m0fnu` (not just `Float4_e2m1fn_x2`), fixing compilation on PyTorch < 2.8.
- Uses `hasattr` checks in `dtype.pxi` to conditionally add `float8_e8m0fnu` and `float4_e2m1fn_x2` entries to `TORCH_DTYPE_TO_DTYPE` map.
