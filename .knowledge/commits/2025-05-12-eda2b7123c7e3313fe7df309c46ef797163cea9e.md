# Fix clang20 compilation error by qualifying nullptr_t with std:: in String constructor
## TL;DR
- Changes `String(nullptr_t)` to `String(std::nullptr_t)` in `include/tvm/ffi/string.h` to fix a compilation error with clang 20.
- The deleted constructor prevents implicit construction of `String` from `nullptr`; the fix is purely a namespace qualification to satisfy stricter compiler requirements.
