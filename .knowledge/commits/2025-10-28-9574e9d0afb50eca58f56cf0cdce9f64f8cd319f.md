# Fix ml_dtypes<0.5 compatibility by gating newer dtype attributes behind hasattr check
## TL;DR
- Moves 8 `ml_dtypes` dtype entries (`int2`, `uint2`, `float8_e3m4`, `float8_e4m3`, `float8_e8m0fnu`, `float6_e2m3fn`, `float6_e3m2fn`, `float4_e2m1fn`) from the unconditional `MLDTYPES_DTYPE_TO_DTYPE` dict literal into a conditional block gated by `hasattr(ml_dtypes, "int2")`.
- These types were introduced in `ml_dtypes>=0.5.0` and caused `AttributeError` on `ml_dtypes==0.4.x`.
- The 7 types available in both `ml_dtypes<0.5` and `>=0.5` (`int4`, `uint4`, `bfloat16`, `float8_e4m3b11fnuz`, `float8_e4m3fn`, `float8_e4m3fnuz`, `float8_e5m2`, `float8_e5m2fnuz`) remain in the base dict.
