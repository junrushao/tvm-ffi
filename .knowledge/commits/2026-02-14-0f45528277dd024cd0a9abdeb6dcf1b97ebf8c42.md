# Suppress GCC -Warray-bounds false positive in String::Concat
## TL;DR
- Adds `#pragma GCC diagnostic ignored "-Warray-bounds"` alongside existing `-Wstringop-overflow` and `-Wrestrict` suppressions in `string.h`
- Fixes a GCC false positive warning in `String::Concat` where `memcpy` offset is reported as out of bounds
