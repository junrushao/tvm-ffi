# Suppress false-positive GCC -Warray-bounds warning in String::Concat
## TL;DR
- Adds `#pragma GCC diagnostic ignored "-Warray-bounds"` inside the existing `#pragma GCC diagnostic push/pop` block in `include/tvm/ffi/string.h` around `String::Concat`'s `memcpy` call.
- GCC (not clang) incorrectly infers negative offset bounds when `String::Concat` is inlined into callers with value-typed `String` local variables, triggering a spurious warning.
