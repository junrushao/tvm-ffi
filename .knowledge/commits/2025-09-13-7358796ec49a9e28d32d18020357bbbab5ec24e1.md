# Windows compatibility fixups: CMake modernization, MSVC SFINAE fix, and comment encoding cleanup

## TL;DR

- Replaced deprecated `FetchContent_Populate` + manual `add_subdirectory` with `FetchContent_MakeAvailable` in `cmake/Utils/AddGoogleTest.cmake` (modern CMake, avoids Windows-specific populate issues).
- Fixed MSVC SFINAE compile error in `Tuple` constructor by changing `std::remove_cv_t<UTypes>` → `std::decay_t<UTypes>` — `remove_cv_t` does not strip references, causing MSVC to reject the template under certain overload resolution paths.
- Replaced non-ASCII em-dash (`–`) with ASCII dash (`-`) in `json_parser.cc` comments, and changed `RUNTIME_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` to `lib` (from `bin`) in `tests/cpp/CMakeLists.txt` while removing the `MSVC_RUNTIME_LIBRARY` property.
