# Fix comments and style in reflection init/ObjectDef
## TL;DR
- Modernize `reflection::init` default constructor to `constexpr noexcept = default`.
- Add `[[maybe_unused]]` to `init_func` parameter in `ObjectDef::def()` to suppress unused-parameter warnings.
- Rename private constant `INIT_METHOD_NAME` to `kInitMethodName` (Google C++ style).
