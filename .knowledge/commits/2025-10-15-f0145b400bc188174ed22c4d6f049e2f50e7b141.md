# Fix comments and naming conventions in reflection init/ObjectDef
## TL;DR
- Renames private constant `INIT_METHOD_NAME` to `kInitMethodName` to follow Google C++ naming conventions.
- Changes `init()` default constructor from empty-body `{}` to `constexpr noexcept = default`.
- Adds `[[maybe_unused]]` attribute to the `init_func` parameter in `ObjectDef::def()` to suppress compiler warnings.
