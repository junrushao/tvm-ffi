# Introduce load_lib_module API for downstream library loading
## TL;DR
- Adds `tvm_ffi.libinfo.load_lib_module(package, target)` that simplifies downstream library loading to a 2-line pattern.
- Simplifies the packaging example (`examples/packaging/python/my_ffi_extension/base.py`) from 27 to 1 line.
- Works correctly for both editable and non-editable builds.
