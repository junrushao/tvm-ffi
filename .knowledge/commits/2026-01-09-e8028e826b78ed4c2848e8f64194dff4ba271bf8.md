- **Python bindings** (`0012-ffi-python-package.md`): Documents `@register_object`, `tvm-ffi-stubgen`, Python cross-language lifetime semantics.
- **C ABI** (`0001-ffi-c-abi.md`): Documents `TVMFFIObject` struct layout, `TVMFFIObjectIncRef`/`TVMFFIObjectDecRef`, `kTVMFFIStaticObjectBegin`.
