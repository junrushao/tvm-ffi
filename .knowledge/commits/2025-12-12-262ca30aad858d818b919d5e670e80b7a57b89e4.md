# Fix clang-tidy warnings in tensor header, source, and tests
## TL;DR
- Add `std::move` to `TensorObjFromNDAlloc` constructor initializer lists and `ViewNDAlloc` constructor to avoid unnecessary copies of allocator objects.
- Mark `ViewNDAlloc` single-argument constructor `explicit` to prevent implicit conversions.
- Cast `prototype->ndim` (int) to `size_t` via `static_cast` and switch test loop variables from `int64_t` to `size_t` to silence signed/unsigned comparison warnings.
