# Fix clang-tidy warnings in tensor allocator constructors and test loop

## TL;DR
- `TensorObjFromNDAlloc` constructors and `ViewNDAlloc` constructor now `std::move` their `TNDAlloc`/`ObjectPtr` arguments to avoid redundant copies.
- `TVMFFITensorCreateUnsafeView`: `prototype->ndim * 2` cast to `static_cast<size_t>(prototype->ndim) * 2` to silence signed/unsigned mismatch warning.
- Two test loops in `test_tensor.cc` changed from `int64_t` index to `size_t` to match `GetDataSize`-derived capacity, with an added `ASSERT_EQ` sanity check.
