# Fix clang-tidy warnings in tensor allocation and test code
## TL;DR
- Add `std::move` to by-value allocator parameters in `TensorObjFromNDAlloc` constructors and `ViewNDAlloc` member initializer to avoid unnecessary copies (clang-tidy performance-move-constructor-init / misc-move-forwarding-reference).
- Mark `ViewNDAlloc` constructor `explicit` to prevent implicit single-argument conversion (google-explicit-constructor).
- Fix signed-to-unsigned implicit conversions in `tensor.cc` (`ndim * 2`) and `test_tensor.cc` (loop counters) to suppress sign-conversion warnings.
