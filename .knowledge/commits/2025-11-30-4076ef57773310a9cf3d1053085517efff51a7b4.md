# Work around CI segfaults with clang-tidy suppression and explicit module cleanup in test
## TL;DR
- Adds a `NOLINTNEXTLINE(clang-analyzer-cplusplus.NewDeleteLeaks)` annotation to `unpack_call` in `function_details.h` to suppress a false-positive clang-tidy warning about memory leaks in the packed-function unpacking path.
- Adds explicit `gc.collect()` + `del mod` at the end of `test_stl` in `tests/python/test_stl.py` to ensure the loaded module is destroyed deterministically, working around segfaults observed in CI after PR #228.
