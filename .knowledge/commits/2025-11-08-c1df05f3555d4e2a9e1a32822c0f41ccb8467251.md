# Streamline number handling with custom protocols
## TL;DR
- Introduces two custom protocols (`__tvm_ffi_int__`, `__tvm_ffi_float__`) for number dispatch in `cython/function.pxi`, moving `numbers.Integral` and `numbers.Real` handling to a more conservative fallback path.
- Adds 4 new lines to `_convert.py` for protocol registration.
- Comprehensive tests added (33 new lines in `test_function.py`).
