# Fix missing dl and pthread link dependencies for tvm_ffi shared/static targets
## TL;DR
- Adds `Threads::Threads` (pthread) and `${CMAKE_DL_LIBS}` (dl) as link dependencies for `tvm_ffi_shared` and `tvm_ffi_static`, fixing linker errors on systems where these are not implicitly linked.
- `std::mutex` usage in `backtrace.cc` requires pthread; `dlsym`/`dlopen` in `library_module_dynamic_lib.cc` requires dl.
