# fix(build): Link dl and pthread dependencies for tvm_ffi shared and static targets

## TL;DR
- Added `find_package(Threads REQUIRED)` and linked `Threads::Threads` into `tvm_ffi_shared` (PRIVATE) and `tvm_ffi_static` (INTERFACE)
- Added linking of `${CMAKE_DL_LIBS}` (libdl on Linux) into both targets when `TVM_FFI_USE_EXTRA_CXX_API` is ON
