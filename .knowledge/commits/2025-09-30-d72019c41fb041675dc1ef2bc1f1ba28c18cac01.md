# Set Sphinx right-sidebar TOC expansion depth to 2 for better discoverability
## TL;DR
- Adds `"show_toc_level": 2` to `html_theme_options` in `docs/conf.py`, causing the right-sidebar table of contents to auto-expand two levels deep instead of the default one.
