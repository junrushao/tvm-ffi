# Move macOS cp38 skip from CI env var to pyproject.toml
## TL;DR
- Removes the `CIBW_SKIP: "cp38-macosx_*"` environment variable from the GitHub Actions wheel-publishing workflow.
- Adds `"cp38-macosx_*"` to the `skip` list in the `[tool.cibuildwheel]` section of `pyproject.toml`, consolidating all skip rules in one place.
