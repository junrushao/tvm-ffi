# Switch wheel test trigger from push-to-main to nightly schedule

## TL;DR
- Removes `push: branches: [main]` trigger from `publish_wheel.yml` (added in 9d54755).
- Replaces it with a `schedule:` trigger (nightly cron) to reduce CI load while still validating wheels regularly.
