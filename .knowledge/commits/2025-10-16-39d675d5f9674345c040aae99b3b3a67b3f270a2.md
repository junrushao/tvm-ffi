# Switch wheel builds from push-on-main to daily nightly cron schedule
## TL;DR
- Replace `push: branches: [main]` trigger with `schedule: cron: '0 9 * * *'` (daily 9am UTC) for wheel building.
- Remove the `if` condition that restricted `manylinux2014` matrix entries to manual `workflow_dispatch` only -- all matrix entries now build on both schedule and manual triggers.
- Remove cosmetic `name:` field from `build_wheels` job.
