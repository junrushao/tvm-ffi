# Add cmake-format and cmake-lint pre-commit hooks and bulk-reformat all CMake files

## TL;DR
- Adds `cmake-format` and `cmake-lint` hooks (v0.6.13) from `cheshirekow/cmake-format-precommit` to `.pre-commit-config.yaml`.
- Introduces `.cmake-format.json` config: 100-col line width, 2-space tabs, `dangle_parens=true`, `command_case=canonical`, space-separated control keywords (`endif ()`, `else ()`).
- Bulk-reformats all CMake files (`CMakeLists.txt`, `cmake/Utils/*.cmake`, `examples/*/CMakeLists.txt`, `tests/cpp/CMakeLists.txt`) to comply; minor variable rename: `tvm_ffi_objs_sources` → `_tvm_ffi_objs_sources` and `tvm_ffi_extra_objs_sources` → `_tvm_ffi_extra_objs_sources` (local variable underscore-prefix convention enforced by cmake-lint).
