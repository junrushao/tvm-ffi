# Fix compiler warnings: remove trailing semicolons after TVM_FFI_DLL_EXPORT_TYPED_FUNC and add [[maybe_unused]] attribute
## TL;DR
- Remove trailing semicolons after all `TVM_FFI_DLL_EXPORT_TYPED_FUNC(...)` macro invocations across examples, tests, and src (11 call sites in 10 files). The macro already expands to a complete declaration; the extra semicolon triggered `-Wextra-semi` warnings.
- Add `[[maybe_unused]]` to the `num_args` parameter of `TypedOverload::GetMismatchMessageAux` in `include/tvm/ffi/reflection/overload.h` to suppress `-Wunused-parameter` when the recursive template bottoms out at `I >= kNumArgs`.
