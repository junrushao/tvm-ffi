# Hide symbols from libbacktrace in CMake build
## TL;DR
- Added `-fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden` to libbacktrace CFLAGS in `cmake/Utils/AddLibbacktrace.cmake` for GCC/Clang/AppleClang, preventing internal libbacktrace symbols from being exported in `libtvm_ffi.so`.
