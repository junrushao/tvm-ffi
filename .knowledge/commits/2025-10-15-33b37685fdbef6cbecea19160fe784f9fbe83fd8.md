# Hide libbacktrace symbols from the shared library's public symbol table on GCC/Clang

## TL;DR
- Adds `-fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden` compile flags to the libbacktrace `ExternalProject_Add` invocation in `cmake/Utils/AddLibbacktrace.cmake`.
- Flags are conditioned on `CMAKE_C_COMPILER_ID` matching `GNU|Clang|AppleClang`; MSVC builds are unaffected.
- Prevents internal libbacktrace symbols from leaking into `libtvm_ffi_shared`'s exported symbol table, reducing ABI surface and avoiding symbol collisions with other libraries that link libbacktrace.
