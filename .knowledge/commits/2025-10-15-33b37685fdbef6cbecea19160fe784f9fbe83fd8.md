# Hide symbols from libbacktrace by adding -fvisibility=hidden to ExternalProject CFLAGS
## TL;DR
- Adds `-fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden` to the `CFLAGS` passed to the libbacktrace `ExternalProject_Add` configure step in `cmake/Utils/AddLibbacktrace.cmake`.
- The flags are conditional on GCC, Clang, or AppleClang compilers (`CMAKE_C_COMPILER_ID` check).
- Prevents internal libbacktrace symbols from leaking into the public symbol table of `libtvm_ffi.so`, reducing symbol pollution and potential conflicts with other libraries.

## Key Exports
None

## Impact
- API: none
- Behavioral: Internal libbacktrace symbols are now hidden in the compiled static library, so they do not appear in the dynamic symbol table of `libtvm_ffi.so` when the static archive is linked in. No user-visible behavior change.
- Flags/config: New `symbol_hiding_flags` CMake variable (local to `_libbacktrace_compile` function) conditionally set to `-fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden`.
- Data formats/schemas: none
