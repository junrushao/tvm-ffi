# Add forward declaration of Tensor class in tensor.h to fix MSVC compilation

## TL;DR
- Adds `class Tensor;` forward declaration near the top of `include/tvm/ffi/container/tensor.h`, before functions that reference `Tensor` before its definition.
- No functional change; fixes MSVC strict forward-declaration enforcement that GCC/Clang tolerate.
