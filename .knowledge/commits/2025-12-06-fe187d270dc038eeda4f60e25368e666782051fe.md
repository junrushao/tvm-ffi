# Fix clang-tidy warnings in test_c_env_api.cc: C-style casts, lock_guard, and vector reserve
## TL;DR
- Replace all C-style casts (`(int*)h`, `(ThreadSafeCounter*)h`) with `static_cast<>` to satisfy clang-tidy `cppcoreguidelines-pro-type-cstyle-cast`.
- Replace `std::lock_guard` with `std::scoped_lock` to satisfy clang-tidy `modernize-use-scoped-lock`.
- Add `threads.reserve(num_threads)` before thread vector population to satisfy clang-tidy `performance-inefficient-vector-operation`.
