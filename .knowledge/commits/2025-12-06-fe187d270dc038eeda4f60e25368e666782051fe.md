# Fix clang-tidy warnings in test_c_env_api.cc: static_cast, scoped_lock, reserve
## TL;DR
- Replaced C-style casts (`(int*)h`, `(ThreadSafeCounter*)h`) with `static_cast<>` in `tests/cpp/extra/test_c_env_api.cc`.
- Replaced `std::lock_guard<std::mutex>` with `std::scoped_lock<std::mutex>` in multithreaded test sections.
- Added `threads.reserve(num_threads)` before thread vector population to avoid reallocations.
