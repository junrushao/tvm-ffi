# Fix clang-tidy warnings in testing helpers and metadata test forward declarations
## TL;DR
- Change two parameters in `schema_arr_map_opt` from pass-by-value to `const&` to fix `performance-unnecessary-value-param` clang-tidy warnings (`src/ffi/testing/testing.cc`).
- Add `NOLINTNEXTLINE(bugprone-reserved-identifier)` to three `extern "C"` forward declarations of `__tvm_ffi__` symbols in `tests/cpp/test_metadata.cc` to suppress false-positive clang-tidy warnings about reserved double-underscore identifiers.

## Design Elements
Design elements this commit consumes:
- **FFI symbol prefix convention** (`__tvm_ffi__` double-underscore for internal symbols) -- [ADR 0010](../ADRs/0010-ffi-symbol-prefix-convention.md). The `bugprone-reserved-identifier` warnings are an inherent tension: the C++ standard reserves `__`-prefixed identifiers, but the FFI ABI deliberately uses them. `NOLINTNEXTLINE` is the established suppression pattern.
- **Container types** (`Array`, `Map`, `Optional`, `String`) -- [design 0007](../designs/0007-containers.md). The `const&` change applies to `Array<Optional<int64_t>>` and `Optional<String>` parameters.
