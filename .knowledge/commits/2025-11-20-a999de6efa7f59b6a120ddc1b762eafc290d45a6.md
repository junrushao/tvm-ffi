# Fix clang-tidy warnings in testing.cc and test_metadata.cc

## TL;DR
- Changed two `schema_arr_map_opt` parameters from pass-by-value to pass-by-const-ref (`Array<Optional<int64_t>>` and `Optional<String>`) to satisfy clang-tidy performance warnings.
- Added `// NOLINTNEXTLINE(bugprone-reserved-identifier)` suppressions for three `__tvm_ffi__*` and `__doc_*` extern C forward declarations in `test_metadata.cc` whose double-underscore names are reserved identifiers in C/C++ but intentionally used for the FFI symbol naming convention.
