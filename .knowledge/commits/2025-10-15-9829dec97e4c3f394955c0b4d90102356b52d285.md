# Remove obsolete DLPack typedefs from c_api.h and rename DLPackTensorAllocator to DLPackManagedTensorAllocator

## TL;DR
- Removed the `DLPackTensorAllocator` typedef and the `DLManagedTensorVersioned` forward declaration from `c_api.h` -- these are now provided by the updated upstream `dlpack.h`.
- Renamed `DLPackTensorAllocator` to `DLPackManagedTensorAllocator` across C++, Python/Cython, and Rust layers to align with the latest DLPack naming.
- Removed obsolete `DLPackFromPyObject` and `DLPackToPyObject` C-style function pointer typedefs from `tvm_ffi_python_helpers.h` and `base.pxi` (replaced by the `DLPackExchangeAPI` struct protocol).
- Fixed unused variable warning in Rust (`Ok(m)` -> `Ok(_m)`) in `tvm-ffi-macros/src/utils.rs`.

## Impact
- API: Breaking rename of `DLPackTensorAllocator` -> `DLPackManagedTensorAllocator` in C++, Rust, and Cython layers. All callers passing or storing this type must update. The `DLPackFromPyObject` and `DLPackToPyObject` typedefs are removed entirely (already superseded by `DLPackExchangeAPI`).
- Behavioral: None -- pure rename and dead-code removal.
- Flags/config: None.
- Data formats/schemas: None.
