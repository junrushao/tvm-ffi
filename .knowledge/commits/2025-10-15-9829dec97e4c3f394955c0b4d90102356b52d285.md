# Rename DLPackTensorAllocator to DLPackManagedTensorAllocator and remove obsolete DLPack typedefs

## TL;DR
- Renames `DLPackTensorAllocator` to `DLPackManagedTensorAllocator` across C++, Python/Cython, and Rust layers to align with the upstream DLPack naming convention after migrating to the latest DLPack version.
- Removes the now-redundant `DLPackTensorAllocator` typedef and `DLManagedTensorVersioned` forward declaration from `c_api.h` (these are now provided by the upstream `dlpack.h`).
- Removes obsolete `DLPackFromPyObject`, `DLPackToPyObject`, and `DLPackTensorAllocator` Cython typedefs from `base.pxi` and `tvm_ffi_python_helpers.h`.
