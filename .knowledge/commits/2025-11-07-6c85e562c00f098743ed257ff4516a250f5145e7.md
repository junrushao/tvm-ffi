# Enable compatibility with cuda-python driver stream objects
## TL;DR
- Adds compact mode in `python/tvm_ffi/cython/function.pxi` to treat `cuda_driver.CUstream` arguments as `void*`, since cuda-python driver streams do not yet support the CUDA stream protocol.
- Adds stream compatibility tests.
