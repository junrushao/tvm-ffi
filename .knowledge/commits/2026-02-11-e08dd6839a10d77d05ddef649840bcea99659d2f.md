# Pin ty to 0.0.15, restructure dependency groups, and tighten pre-commit order
## TL;DR
- Pins `ty` to `0.0.15` (from unversioned) and adds `--error-on-warning` to the ty pre-commit hook. Moves the ty hook earlier in `.pre-commit-config.yaml` (before clang-format, after ruff).
- Restructures `pyproject.toml` dependency groups: moves `torch`/`numpy`/`ml_dtypes` out of `[optional-dependencies]` into `[dependency-groups]`; composes `test = [{include-group = "torch"}, "pytest"]` and `dev = [{include-group = "test"}, ...]`. Removes `[project.optional-dependencies]` `test` and `torch` keys.
- Adds `ml_dtypes >= 0.4.0` version guard in `tests/python/test_dtype.py`; removes `python = ".venv"` from `[tool.ty.environment]`; adds `[tool.ty.rules] unused-ignore-comment = "ignore"`.
