# Prevent integer overflow in GetDataSize on 32-bit platforms
## TL;DR
- Casts `numel` to `uint64_t` before multiplying by `dtype.bits * dtype.lanes` in `GetDataSize()` to prevent integer overflow on 32-bit platforms (e.g. WASM).
