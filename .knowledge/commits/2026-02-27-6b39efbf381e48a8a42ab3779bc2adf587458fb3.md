# Consolidate repr_print/deep_copy into dataclass.cc with recursive hash/compare APIs
## TL;DR
- Creates new `src/ffi/extra/dataclass.cc` (~1750 lines) consolidating repr_print, deep_copy, recursive compare and recursive hash into a single dataclass support module.
- Removes standalone `deep_copy.cc` and `repr_print.cc`.
- Adds `include/tvm/ffi/extra/dataclass.h` and `include/tvm/ffi/reflection/init.h` for reflection-based initialization.
- Extends test objects with richer field types for thorough testing.
