# Support CUDA stream protocol (__cuda_stream__) in FFI call dispatch
## TL;DR
- Adds support for the NVIDIA CUDA stream protocol (`__cuda_stream__`) in the Cython arg setter, converting stream objects to `void*` for FFI calls.
- Adds tests for `Device` with cuda stream arguments.

## Impact
- API: Objects implementing `__cuda_stream__` are now auto-converted to void* Device streams in FFI calls.
