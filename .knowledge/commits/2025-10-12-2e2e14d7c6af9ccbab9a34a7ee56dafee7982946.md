# Add sphinx-design as documentation build dependency
## TL;DR
- Adds `sphinx-design` to the Sphinx extensions list in `docs/conf.py` and as a `[docs]` dependency in `pyproject.toml`.
- Updates `docs/.rstcheck.cfg` to ignore new directives (`tab-set`, `collapse`, `tabs`) and roles (`c:macro`) introduced by sphinx-design, and suppresses `cpp`/`python` code-block validation.
