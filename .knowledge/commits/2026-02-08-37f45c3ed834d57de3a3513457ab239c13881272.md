# Fix torch_c_dlpack_ext build reliability: handle missing torch and ABI mismatch
## TL;DR
- Fixes `torch_c_dlpack_ext` build backend to gracefully skip compilation when `torch` is not installed (avoids `ModuleNotFoundError` bailout).
- Catches `AttributeError` from `ctypes.CDLL` when torch_c_dlpack_ext and torch have ABI mismatches (e.g., in NGC containers), falling through to JIT compilation path.
