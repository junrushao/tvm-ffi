# Support load-bearing compiler flags for cross-compilation
## TL;DR
- Adds support for `DLPACK_EXTRA_CFLAGS` and `DLPACK_EXTRA_LDFLAGS` environment variables in `_build_optional_torch_c_dlpack.py`, enabling cross-compilation scenarios (e.g., x64 clang targeting riscv64 with `--sysroot` and `--target`).
