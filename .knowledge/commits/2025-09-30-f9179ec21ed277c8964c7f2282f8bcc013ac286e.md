# Explicitly zero __padding in TVMFFIObject header during allocation
## TL;DR
- Adds `ffi_ptr->__padding = 0;` in both `ObjAllocatorBase::make_object` and `ObjAllocatorBase::make_inplace_array`, zeroing the previously-uninitialized 4-byte padding field in the `TVMFFIObject` header.
- The `__padding` field (uint32_t at offset 12) exists solely for 8-byte alignment between `type_index` (int32) and `deleter` (function pointer). It has no semantic use, but leaving it uninitialized creates non-deterministic memory content.
