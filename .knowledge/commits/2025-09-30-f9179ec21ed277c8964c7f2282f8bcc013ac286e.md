# Explicitly zero-initialize padding in memory allocation

## TL;DR
- Adds `ffi_ptr->__padding = 0;` after `combined_ref_count` initialization in `ObjAllocatorBase::make` and `MakeArray`, ensuring the padding field in `TVMFFIObject` header is zeroed.
