# Zero-initialize __padding in TVMFFIObject header during object allocation
## TL;DR
- Added `ffi_ptr->__padding = 0;` in both `ObjAllocatorBase::make_object` and `ObjAllocatorBase::make_inplace_array` to ensure deterministic memory.
- The `__padding` field in `TVMFFIObject` was previously left uninitialized; while unused, this could cause non-deterministic behavior in memory comparisons or serialization.
