# Enable comprehensive Ruff rule coverage and apply fixes across codebase
## TL;DR
- Configures extensive Ruff lint rules in `pyproject.toml`: UP (pyupgrade), PL (pylint), I (isort), RUF (ruff-specific), NPY (numpy), F (pyflakes), PTH (pathlib), D (pydocstyle), with per-file ignores for tests and `__init__.py`. Removes legacy black and isort tool sections.
- Applies resulting fixes across ~35 Python files: adds Google-style docstrings, replaces `os.path` with `pathlib.Path`, applies pyupgrade transformations (f-strings, `isinstance` unions, etc.), and reformats `pyproject.toml` arrays.
- Removes obsolete `tests/lint/git-clang-format.sh` (93 lines) and `tests/scripts/task_lint.sh` (46 lines), completing migration to pre-commit hooks.
