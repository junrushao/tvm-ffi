# Add gc.collect() and del mod to STL test to prevent segfaults on module unload
## TL;DR
- Adds `gc.collect()` and `del mod` after `run_check(mod)` in `test_stl.py` to force garbage collection of objects holding library deleters before the module is unloaded, complementing the sub-function scoping from commit 5a82940.
