# Make Threads linkage optional in CMake for cross-compilation support
## TL;DR
- Changes `find_package(Threads REQUIRED)` to `find_package(Threads)` (optional) in `CMakeLists.txt` and guards `target_link_libraries` with `if (Threads_FOUND)`, enabling builds on cross-compilation targets where pthreads may be unavailable.
