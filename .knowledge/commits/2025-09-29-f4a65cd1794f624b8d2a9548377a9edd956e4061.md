# Remove stride normalization in torch C DLPack exporter
## TL;DR
- Removes the stride normalization workaround (`as_strided` for scalar-like tensors with `stride(0) != 1`) from the custom torch C DLPack exporter `toDLPackImpl`. The original strides are now passed through unchanged.
