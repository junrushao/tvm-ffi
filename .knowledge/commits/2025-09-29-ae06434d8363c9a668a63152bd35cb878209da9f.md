# Swap argument order in TVM_FFI_CHECK to place condition first
## TL;DR
- Changes `TVM_FFI_CHECK(ErrorKind, cond)` to `TVM_FFI_CHECK(cond, ErrorKind)` for consistency with `TVM_FFI_ICHECK(cond)` and idiomatic condition-first style.

## Impact
- API: Breaking change for any callers of `TVM_FFI_CHECK` (introduced in the previous commit). Condition and ErrorKind arguments are swapped.
